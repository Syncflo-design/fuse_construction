# Site on a Phone

*Crew time, approvals, progress and the daily report — Fuse Construction user guide 06*

Open **Site** from Fuse Home on the phone. Choose the **project** once; the phone remembers it.

## Crew time (foreman)

1. Tap **Crew time**.
2. Check the **date** and choose the **section** the crew worked on.
3. Enter **Hours each** and **Overtime each** — or just the hours: anything past the standard
   day becomes overtime by itself.
4. Tick the crew, or tap **Same crew as …** to tick yesterday's.
5. A worker who did something different: tap their hours and change them, or move them to
   another section. Two rows for one worker split their day.
6. Tap **Send for approval**.

## Approve time (supervisor)

Tap **Approve time**. Each crew sheet shows who worked where and for how long. **Approve**
books the labour to the job (one timesheet per worker, costed at their rate). **Send back**
returns it to the foreman with your reason.

## Progress

Tap **Progress**, then a section, and enter its **% complete to date** — or **Section
complete**. Earned value moves with it.

## Daily report

Weather, labour on site, hours lost, work done (with each section's %), plant, issues and
safety. **Take a photo** uses the phone's camera. **Sign** with a finger and tap **Sign and
submit** — a signed report is locked.

## Material request and deliveries

- **Material request**: search an item, set the quantity, choose the section, send.
- **Receive delivery**: opens the receiving screen where one is installed; otherwise choose
  the order, enter what arrived, and book it in.
