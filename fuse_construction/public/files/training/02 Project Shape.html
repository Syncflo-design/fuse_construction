Project Shape

Budget against committed, actual and earned value — Fuse Construction user guide 02

What it shows

Construction → Project Shape. Choose a project for one job; leave it empty for every job.

For one job:

  • a curve of planned value, earned value, actual cost and committed-plus-actual, week by week (or month by month), up to the date you choose;
  • a table by section: budget, committed, actual, % complete, earned and planned value, variances, CPI, SPI and the forecast at completion;
  • cards across the top: contract value, budget, committed + actual, earned value, CPI, SPI, forecast and health.

Where the numbers come from

Figure Comes from
Budget The awarded BOQ's cost per section, plus approved budget revisions
Committed Open purchase orders not yet received, and subcontract value not yet certified
Actual Goods received, stock issued to the job, subcontract certificates (less contra-charges) and approved crew time
% complete Each section's task — moved from the phone, a daily report or a client valuation
Planned value The budget spread evenly over each section's planned dates
Earned value Budget × % complete

A rand is never in both committed and actual: as an order is received or a subcontract is certified it moves from one to the other.

Reading it

Means
CPI below 1 The work done has cost more than its budget
SPI below 1 Less work is done than the programme planned by now
Forecast at completion What the job will cost if it carries on at its current rate
Health Green: on budget and programme. Amber: slipping. Red: act.

Cost that landed on no section (a receipt without one, say) shows as Unallocated so it is never hidden.

The dashboard

Construction → Dashboard puts one job on a screen: the cards, the curve, sections, subcontracts, the client position and procurement coverage.
