Choosing who builds or supplies a package — Fuse Construction user guide 03

Raising a tender

From an awarded BOQ: Create → Tender, choose the section and whether it is a Subcontract or a Supply package. The scope lines and estimated value come from the BOQ.

Add the bidders you are inviting and the documents, set the submission deadline, and Submit to publish.

Recording bids

On the published tender, fill in each bidder's Bid Amount, Programme (weeks) and the compliance ticks (compliant, tax clearance, B-BBEE level, insurance, COIDA), and set the bid status to Bid Received. Save.

The tender shows the lowest compliant bid: a cheaper bid that fails compliance is not the benchmark.

Press Start Evaluation when the bids are in.

Awarding

Press Award and choose the bidder. Fuse:

  • marks that bid Awarded and the others Not Awarded,
  • for a subcontract package, creates a draft subcontract with the scope priced to the bid;
  • for a supply package, creates a draft purchase order.

The draft is checked and submitted by whoever signs the contract.
