Client Billing

Valuations, milestones and client retention — Fuse Construction user guide 05

How the client is billed

On the BOQ's Client Terms tab, Bill the Client By is either:

  • Measured Valuation — % complete per section, each month; or
  • Milestones — fixed percentages of the contract at agreed points (from the template's programme, or typed in).

The same tab holds the client's advance, retention, retention cap, payment terms and the client's own retention release stages.

A measured valuation

Create → Client Valuation. Each section arrives with the % it reached last time. Enter the % complete to date. Only the movement is billed. Fuse holds back the client's retention (up to its cap) and recovers the client's advance in the same proportion it was paid.

Submitting it moves each section's progress — the same figure earned value is measured from.

A milestone claim

Choose Valuation: Milestone. The open milestones are listed; tick the ones reached.

The advance

Choose Valuation: Advance to invoice the client's advance.

Client retention

Record Practical Completion on the BOQ. Release stages fall due from it. Create → Client Retention Release when a stage is due.

No ERPNext sales invoice is raised: with Intacct posting on, a valuation becomes an AR invoice in Intacct.
